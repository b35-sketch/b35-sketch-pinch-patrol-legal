Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

The short version

Pinch Patrol does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no adverts and no analytics.

What is stored on your device

Your best score, your highest wave reached and your sound preferences are saved on your own device only. They are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data. They are never uploaded to us.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your final score for each round is submitted to the game's leaderboard so you can compare it with other players. This is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your Apple ID or any personal details, and the game is fully playable without signing in.

Network use

The game is designed to work entirely offline. It makes no network requests of its own, uses no third-party SDKs, and contains no tracking technology of any kind.

Children

Pinch Patrol is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
