Frequently asked

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Pinch Patrol plays completely offline. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Where is my high score kept?

Only on your own device. It is removed if you delete the game or clear its data. On iPhone and iPad your score is also sent to the Game Center leaderboard if you are signed in.

How do I turn the sound off?

Pause the game, open Settings, and switch off wave ambience or game sounds.
